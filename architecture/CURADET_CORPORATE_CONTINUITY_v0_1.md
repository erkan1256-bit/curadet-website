Before creating, editing, publishing, coding, designing, or deciding anything, the executing unit MUST resolve these in order:

1. LOCKED PRINCIPLES — applicable CURADET principles and non-negotiable rules.
2. SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH — current source files, repository state, approved assets, versions and live state.
3. LOCKED DECISIONS — prior approved/final decisions that cannot be reopened without Owner instruction.
4. EXISTING ASSETS — reuse approved logo, brand identity, pages, modules, music assets, schemas and workflows instead of recreating them.
5. UNIT BOUNDARY — identify the responsible CURADET unit and required cross-unit handoffs.
6. CURRENT STATE — distinguish DONE / VERIFIED / OPEN / BLOCKED / OWNER REQUIRED.
7. TASK DELTA — perform only what is new, missing, wrong or outdated.

If any required source exists, memory or guesswork MUST NOT replace it.
If a required source cannot be found, mark SOURCE MISSING and do not silently invent a replacement.

## Context-loss failure class
`CONTEXT_BREAK` is a first-class CURADET failure.

It occurs when work begins without checking applicable locked decisions, source-of-truth, approved assets, current implementation or unit boundaries.

Examples:
- redesigning an already approved logo instead of using the approved master;
- creating a parallel page when an existing canonical module should be extended;
- presenting CURADET publicly as an AI-music brand when the public product is CURADET / MUSIC and AI is an internal replaceable tool;
- rebuilding a completed capability because its current source was not checked.

A technically attractive output with CONTEXT_BREAK is NOT accepted as complete.

## Five senses + emotion principle
CURADET's core human-experience principle is to address the whole person: the five senses and human emotion.

Every applicable CURADET experience — music, visual identity, film/video, social content, website, mobile experience, live/physical experience, packaging and future products — MUST be designed by asking how it can create a coherent human experience across:
- HEARING — music, voice, silence, spatial sound, rhythm, timbre and sonic identity;
- SIGHT — image, light, movement, typography, color, composition and visual identity;
- TOUCH — physical/digital interaction, texture, haptics, material feel and interface response where the medium permits;
- SMELL — atmosphere, place, memory and physical brand experiences where the medium genuinely permits;
- TASTE — hospitality, events, collaborations and physical experiences where the medium genuinely permits;
- EMOTION — meaning, memory, anticipation, belonging, curiosity, excitement, calm, longing, joy and other authentic human feelings appropriate to the work.

This principle does NOT require artificial use of all five senses in every medium. A digital post, song or website must not pretend to deliver smell or taste. Instead, each medium uses the senses it can genuinely reach and may evoke cross-sensory memory or imagination through honest artistic design.

Technology remains an invisible enabler. The target is not sensory gimmicks; it is memorable, coherent, human experience.

For public launches and social media, the creative question is therefore not merely “Does it look good?” or “Does it sound good?” but “What does the person see, hear, feel, remember and want to experience next?”

## CURADET — Philosophical Visual Interpretation Principle
This principle applies to CURADET MERKEZ and every unit involved in visual production.

Song and work titles MUST NOT be illustrated merely by depicting the literal meaning of their words. Before visual production begins, the title must be considered deeply in terms of meaning, emotion, association and human condition.

The purpose is not to show the word directly, but to make the thought and feeling it awakens in a person perceptible through an original visual language.

Examples:
- “Sevinçten uçuyorum” does not require an image of a flying person. Joy, lightness and exhilaration may be expressed through different scenes, movement or symbols.
- “Hâlâ Ayakta” does not require an image of a standing person. Endurance, resistance, continuation or renewed strength may be interpreted through different objects, spaces and visual relationships.

Each work MUST be interpreted independently. The same symbols, colors, scenes and ready-made visual formulas must not be repeated by habit. The first idea is not automatically accepted; alternative meanings and visual interpretations must be considered before selecting the expression most faithful to the work.

For an album or single cover, the work title is the starting point for interpretation. The title must come alive visually and the cover should create emotion and curiosity at first encounter.

### Fundamental sentence
“Müzik asıl olandır; kapak görseli müziğin gölgesidir.”

The visual must lead toward the music and MUST NOT overpower it. The approved CURADET logo must retain its approved form and be used as a measured signature, never as a visually intrusive element.

The target is original, carefully crafted work that addresses human senses and emotions and avoids generic repetition. Artificiality, careless detail and ready-template appearance are unacceptable.

### Mandatory visual validation before publication
Before publication, verify:
1. work/song title and spelling;
2. philosophical and emotional relationship between title and visual narrative;
3. avoidance of simplistic literal illustration unless literalism is intentionally the strongest interpretation;
4. originality versus repeated symbols, colors, scenes and templates;
5. typography, placement, hierarchy and composition;
6. approved CURADET logo form, scale and restraint;
7. visual detail integrity and absence of artificial/careless artifacts;
8. compatibility with the Five Senses + Emotion Principle;
9. whether the image directs attention toward the music rather than competing with it.

A visual is NOT complete merely because it looks beautiful. It is complete only when its relationship to the work, the emotion it communicates and all publication details have been verified.
